## 3. Materials & Build Quality Standards

### Core Substrate
* **Action TESA HDMR / Premium MDF:**
  * High-Density Moisture-Resistant (HDMR) engineered wood substrate designed specifically for Indian weather conditions (high humidity in monsoon, dry heat in summer).
  * High screw-holding capacity, borer-proof, and termite-resistant.
  * Unlike cheap particle boards, HDMR does not swell, warp, or crack under moisture.

### Finishing (Automotive-Grade PU Polish)
* **Italian Polyurethane (PU) Polish:**
  * Applied in multiple automated spray coats: primer base, high-build sealer, color coat, and dual-layer protective clear topcoat.
  * Available in **Satin Matte** (velvety, fingerprint-resistant) or **High-Gloss Piano Finish** (mirror-like reflective luxury).
  * Non-yellowing formula resistant to heat, hot tea cups, and common household spills.

### Glass & Vitrines
* **Toughened Safety Glass:**
  * 8mm tempered glass shelves with diamond-polished edges capable of holding heavy porcelain crockery and barware.
  * Available in **Fluted Reeded Glass** (semi-obscured architectural texture) and **Tinted Smoke Grey / Bronze Glass**.

### Ambient Lighting & Electricals
* **Concealed LED Systems:**
  * Warm White (3000K) concealed LED strip lights running in aluminum profiles with milky diffusers for glare-free illumination.
  * Smart touch-sensor switches integrated seamlessly into dressing table mirrors with continuous touch dimming.

### Hardware & Fittings
* **Heavy-Duty Soft-Close Hardware:**
  * Hydraulic soft-close hinges tested for 50,000 opening cycles.
  * Silent telescopic ball-bearing drawer slides (full-extension).
  * Premium decorative profile handles in brushed gold, rose gold, and matte black anodized finishes.
